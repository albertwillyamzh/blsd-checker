Supported Pathology Classes

The RBFNN model is trained to recognize four primary foliage categories from leaf imagery.

Healthy

Healthy

Normal banana leaf foliage without necrotic spots, streak lesions, or fungal discoloration.

Sigatoka

Sigatoka

Yellow and dark brown streak lesions advancing into leaf necrosis caused by fungal pathogens.

Pestalotiopsis

Pestalotiopsis

Greyish-brown leaf spot lesions with dark brown margins impairing photosynthetic capacity.

Cordana

Cordana

Large oval or diamond-shaped brown leaf spots surrounded by distinct bright yellow halos.
